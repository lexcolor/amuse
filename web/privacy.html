Amuse Privacy Policy

Effective October 27, 2026

Amuse is a music player that streams the music you already keep in your own cloud storage (Google Drive and Microsoft OneDrive) and plays internet radio. It is developed and operated by lexdes.com ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information Amuse accesses, how it is used, and the choices you have. It applies to the web app at amuse.lexdes.com and to the Amuse desktop apps provided in the open source repo.

In short

  • Amuse only reads your cloud storage. It never uploads, changes, moves, shares or deletes your files.
  • Amuse has no user accounts and no database. We don't collect, store, sell or share your files or information about them.
  • Your sign-in stays on your device. You can disconnect at any time.
  • There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Information Amuse accesses

Google Drive

When you choose Sign in with Google, Amuse asks for one permission: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.readonly ("See and download all your Google Drive files"). Google Drive doesn't offer a narrower permission that lets an app browse the folders you already have and play the audio files in them, so Amuse requests this read-only one. With it, Amuse accesses:

  • File and folder information: names, IDs, file types and shortcuts, so it can show your folders and find your music.
  • The contents of audio files, only when you play a song (to stream it) and, in the desktop apps, the beginning of a file to read its song title, artist, album and cover art.

Amuse only lists folders and files that are audio. It doesn't open documents, photos or other kinds of files.

Microsoft OneDrive

When you choose to sign in with Microsoft, Amuse asks for Files.Read (read your files), User.Read (sign you in) and offline_access (stay signed in). It reads folder and file information, including the song details OneDrive provides, and streams audio files when you play them.

How Amuse uses this information

Only to provide the features you see: showing your music folders, displaying song details and artwork, and playing what you choose. Amuse doesn't use your data for advertising, profiling, or to develop, improve or train artificial intelligence or machine learning models. No person reads your data.

Where your information is kept

  • Sign-in tokens. After you sign in, Google or Microsoft give Amuse a token that lets it keep reading your files. It is stored only on your device: in the browser's storage for the web app, or in your operating system's secure storage (such as Windows Credential Manager or the macOS Keychain) for the desktop apps. Short-lived access tokens are kept in memory.
  • Desktop cache. The desktop apps keep a local cache of song details, cover art and lyrics on your computer so they don't have to be downloaded again. It never leaves your computer.
  • Settings, such as "Music folder only", are stored on your device.

We operate no servers that store your files, file information or tokens.

How your music reaches you

Amuse connects directly from your device to Google, Microsoft and the other services named below.

There is one exception. Web browsers can't attach the required Google sign-in to audio playback, so when the web app plays a song from Google Drive, the request goes through a small relay on our hosting provider, Cloudflare. The relay adds your temporary access token (valid for about an hour) to the request, passes it to Google, and streams the audio back to you as it arrives. It doesn't save the audio, the token or the file information, and we don't log them. Cloudflare processes this traffic as our infrastructure provider under the Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

Sharing

We don't sell, rent, share or transfer information received from Google or Microsoft to anyone. The only exceptions are where you ask for it (for example, by playing a song), where needed for security or to comply with the law.

Amuse's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Other services Amuse talks to

Some features contact free public services. These services receive your IP address and the details listed below, and their own privacy policies apply. None of them receive your files or your Google or Microsoft information.

  • Album art (desktop apps): when a song has no embedded cover, the artist and album name are sent to Deezer and Apple's iTunes Search to find one.
  • Lyrics (desktop apps, off by default): if you turn on "Find lyrics online", the song title and artist are sent to LRCLIB (lrclib.net).
  • Radio: station searches, including a country you pick, go to the Radio Browser directory (radio-browser.info). If you use "Near me" and allow location access, your position is rounded to about 1 km on your device before it is sent. Playing a station connects you directly to that station's server.
  • Samples: the built-in sample songs stream from filesamples.com and soundhelix.com.

Your choices

  • Sign out of Google Drive or OneDrive in Amuse. This deletes the stored sign-in from your device.
  • Revoke access at any time from your Google Account permissions or Microsoft account app permissions.
  • Delete local data by clearing the site's data in your browser, removing the web app from your home screen, or uninstalling the desktop app.

Because we don't hold your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. If you have a question or a request, contact us below.

Security

All connections use HTTPS. Sign-in uses OAuth 2.0 with PKCE, and Amuse never sees your password.

Children

Amuse isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update it on this page and change the effective date. If a change affects how Amuse uses Google or Microsoft information, we'll ask for your consent before applying it.
